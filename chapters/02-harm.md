# 2. Why Does the Platform Care?

<p class="chapter-subtitle">Translate “abuse” into observable ecosystem harm before choosing enforcement.</p>

<div class="chapter-meta"><span>Status: brief</span><span>Part I</span><span>Decision: what to protect</span></div>

## Reader outcome

Build a harm model that identifies affected parties, causal pathways, magnitude, distribution, and reversibility.

## Scope

Unfair competition, degraded experience, scarcity capture, marketplace distortion, spam, fraud, account takeover, artificial engagement, infrastructure cost, reputation, regulatory exposure, and loss of trust.

## Decision questions

- Is the harm direct, externalized, cumulative, or speculative?
- Who benefits from the behavior and who bears its cost?
- Which proxy metrics might be mistaken for harm itself?
- Could an intervention create greater user or ecosystem harm?

## Evidence needed

- Platform transparency and incident reports
- Economic and user-experience measurements
- Longitudinal evidence connecting behavior to outcomes
- Counterexamples where automation improves the ecosystem
