# 5. Policy, Contracts, and Law

<p class="chapter-subtitle">Align technical enforcement with actual authority, disclosure, privacy, and user rights.</p>

<div class="chapter-meta"><span>Status: brief</span><span>Part II</span><span>Decision: what may be enforced</span></div>

## Reader outcome

Recognize when an engineering design requires policy, privacy, security, consumer-protection, employment, or jurisdiction-specific legal review.

## Decision questions

- What conduct do the rules actually prohibit and what remedy do they authorize?
- What happens to purchased goods, balances, or livelihoods after enforcement?
- Which telemetry is necessary, disclosed, proportionate, and retainable?
- What evidence and explanation rights apply in relevant jurisdictions?

## Research boundary

This chapter is an engineering decision guide, not legal advice. Claims must be jurisdiction- and date-specific, sourced primarily to statutes, regulators, decisions, and current platform terms.

## Evidence needed

- Primary legal and regulatory material
- Versioned contracts and enforcement policies
- Privacy impact and retention frameworks
- Documented disputes and remedies
