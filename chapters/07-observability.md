# 7. What Can We Actually Know?

<p class="chapter-subtitle">Inventory evidence by necessity, reliability, manipulability, cost, and retention risk.</p>

<div class="chapter-meta"><span>Status: brief</span><span>Part III</span><span>Decision: what to observe</span></div>

## Reader outcome

Create a signal register that distinguishes direct observations from derived features and inferred claims.

## Recurring signal test

```text
Can we collect it?      Should we collect it?
How reliable is it?     How can attackers manipulate it?
What does it cost?      How long may and should we retain it?
```

## Scope

Server events, network metadata, client telemetry, device signals, session history, transactions, interaction sequences, account relationships, payments, and support contacts.

## Evidence needed

- Data lineage and quality failures
- Privacy and minimization practice
- Signal spoofing and population drift
- Retention and access-control models
