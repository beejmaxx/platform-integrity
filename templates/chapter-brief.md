## Reader outcome

After this chapter, the reader can make or review one concrete decision.

## 1. What is the problem?

Define terms, scope, actors, and the tempting but incomplete framing.

## 2. Why does it matter?

Identify harmed parties, ecosystem consequences, urgency, and error asymmetry.

## 3. What approaches are available?

Compare mechanisms without collapsing policy into technical capability.

## 4. What tradeoffs exist?

Cover evidence quality, privacy, friction, cost, latency, reversibility, law, and adaptation.

## 5. How should an engineer decide?

Give a decision procedure, thresholds for escalation, and residual uncertainty.
